- **R2 — permits-portal**: "A city permits office wants a public portal where residents check the
  status of their permit applications and submit requested documents. Occasional use, all ages,
  desktop and phone."
  - Answer key: expected pattern portals (status-tracker) + record-view-lite; recipe Institutional
    Modern; density 2; must-have: plain-language status milestones, prominent single call-to-action,
    accessibility-safe contrast, phone stacking.
